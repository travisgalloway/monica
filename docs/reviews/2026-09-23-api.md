## 2. Findings

Ranked by severity, then by verdict, with CONFIRMED before PLAUSIBLE.

### High

**API-766a6357** · `scripts/cloud_pod.py:941-955` · rule `non-idempotent-mutation` · severity
high · verdict CONFIRMED

The `launch` handler calls `RunPodClient.create_pod(...)` unconditionally. No prior lookup checks
for an existing pod under the same name. No idempotency key is sent to RunPod. A caller cannot
always tell whether a first `launch` call succeeded, for example after a timeout. A retried
`launch` then provisions a second billable GPU pod under the same default name.

Disposition: ticketed as #441.

**API-37ad2d61** · `scripts/cloud_pod.py:449-458,474-480,537-559` · rule `unvalidated-input` ·
severity high · verdict CONFIRMED

In the default `graphql` API mode, `stop_pod`, `terminate_pod`, and `create_pod` interpolate
several fields into a GraphQL mutation string. None of it is escaped. The fields are `pod_id`,
`name`, `image_name`, `gpu_type_id`, `cloud_type`, `ports`, and hand-built environment key/value
pairs. None of the corresponding CLI arguments carry `choices=` or regex validation. A value
containing a double quote or a brace can alter the mutation sent under the caller's own
credentials.

Disposition: ticketed as #442.

### Low

**API-135e99ae** · `scripts/*.py` · rule `naming-inconsistent` · severity low · verdict CONFIRMED

Of 767 `add_argument()` calls, the output-path concept is spelled three ways. `--out` appears in
35 files, the majority convention. `--output` appears in 13 files: the `eval_*.py` family,
`ablation_sweep.py`, and the `run_*_moe.py` drivers. `--out-dir` appears in three files:
`build_rlvr_prompts.py`, `build_math_rlvr_prompts.py`, and `train_critic.py`. Sixteen of 51 files
that take an output-path flag deviate from the majority spelling.

Disposition: recorded under Decisions needed, below.

## 3. Decisions needed
